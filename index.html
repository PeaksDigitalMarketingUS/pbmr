Our Commitment

Licensed. Vetted. Permitted.
Every Time.

A significant compliance problem exists across the water damage restoration industry. Many mitigation companies perform reconstruction work — drywall, insulation, electrical, plumbing — without holding an ICC general contractor's license or pulling required permits. In many cases, this is illegal. It exposes property owners to code violations, insurance complications, and liability for unpermitted work.

ICC-Licensed Reconstruction

All reconstruction within the PBMR network is performed by a general contractor holding an ICC license — the standard required for drywall, insulation, electrical, and plumbing finish work.

All Permits Pulled

Every reconstruction job is fully permitted through the appropriate local authority. No shortcuts. No unpermitted work. No surprises for property owners or their insurance carriers.

Vetted Network Partners

Every company in the PBMR network is vetted for years of experience, proper licensing, and professional reputation before they receive a single referral.

Full Accountability

Because PBMR coordinates the entire process, there is a single point of accountability for the property owner's complete restoration journey — from the burst pipe to the final coat of paint.
